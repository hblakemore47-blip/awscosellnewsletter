If you're selling with AWS, there's one golden rule: **if it's not in ACE, it doesn't exist to AWS sellers.**

ACE — the APN Customer Engagements tool — is AWS's internal CRM for tracking partner-led opportunities. It's the system AWS account managers live in every day, and it's how they decide who to work with, which deals to support, and where to invest co-sell resources.

Fail to use ACE effectively, and you're invisible. Use it well, and you'll unlock AWS mindshare, funding programs, and Marketplace momentum.

## How ACE Adds Value

**1. Visibility to AWS sellers**
Well-crafted ACE submissions build trust and credibility with AWS field teams. This leads to warmer intros, faster responses, and higher co-sell engagement.

**2. Marketplace "Propensity to Buy" scores**
When an opportunity is accepted, ACE surfaces AWS Marketplace Propensity to Buy scores — these reveal customers most likely to transact on Marketplace.

**3. Multi-partner deal building**
Through Partner Connections, you can team up with other ISVs and SIs, creating stronger, multi-partner solutions that align with AWS's go-to-market priorities.

**4. Improved internal matching**
A strong "Customer Solution" field in your ACE submission helps AWS's internal tools match you to the right account managers and customers.

## Bottom Line

ACE isn't just an admin step — it's your direct line into AWS's sales engine. If you want AWS sellers bringing you into deals, unlocking funding, and championing your solution, it starts with consistent, high-quality ACE engagement.
