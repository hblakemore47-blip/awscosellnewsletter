*A plain-English breakdown for early-stage startups*

If you've been poking around the AWS Partner Network lately, chances are you've heard whispers (or shouts) about Seller Prime — AWS's new initiative to boost high-potential ISVs by giving them fast-track access to Marketplace traction and co-sell alignment.

But what exactly is Seller Prime? And is it something your startup should care about right now?

## What Is Seller Prime?

In simple terms, Seller Prime is a curated track within the AWS ecosystem designed to surface ISVs with a strong product-market fit and get them in front of AWS sellers faster. Think of it as a kind of "early admission" program — but you still have to show your work.

If you're accepted, you'll get:
- Streamlined listing support for AWS Marketplace
- Direct access to co-sell enablement resources
- A signal boost with field sellers across AWS
- The chance to unlock funding opportunities sooner

## How Do You Qualify?

There's no public checklist, but based on conversations with AWS field teams and Seller Prime alumni, here's what tends to matter:
- You're already working with AWS customers
- You have a clear "Better Together" story that maps to AWS services
- You're Marketplace-ready (or already listed)
- You have internal resources to act on AWS co-sell momentum

**Translation:** AWS wants to invest in partners who are ready to scale, not still figuring out where AWS fits in.

## The Reality for Startups

Here's the part you won't find in a datasheet:

Even with Seller Prime status, you still have to do the work — and a lot of the value depends on what you bring to the table.

- It's not a magic pipeline button
- It's a lever — but only if you're ready to pull it

Startups that win with Seller Prime usually:
- Come prepared with seller-ready collateral
- Have someone internally (or fractional) who knows how to navigate ACE and Marketplace
- Actively engage AWS teams with structured GTM plans

## Should You Go for It?

If you're an early-stage startup with:
- A product already gaining traction on AWS
- A strong use case aligned to cloud spend
- The capacity to run a lean co-sell motion

…then Seller Prime could absolutely accelerate your journey.

But if you're still early in your AWS GTM story, focus first on nailing your Better Together narrative and getting Marketplace-ready. That's the foundation Seller Prime is built on.
